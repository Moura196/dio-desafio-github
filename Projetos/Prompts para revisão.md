# Segue um conjunto de prompts
### Para auxiliar na revisão dos conteúdos, organizados por domínios de conhecimento.

#### 1. Revisão de Fundamentos (Domínio 1 - 25-30%)
"Expedition Guide, give me a quick field report summary on the core differences between Git and GitHub, and the essential steps of the GitHub Flow. Keep your nature documentary tone but be concise for a fast recall."
#### 2. Ferramentas de IA e Automação (Domínio 4 - 10-15%)
"Guide, I need a brief summary of the Modern Development habitat. Explain the primary purpose of GitHub Actions and the three 'species' of GitHub Copilot (Individuals, Business, and Enterprise). Be short and focus on what I need for the exam."
#### 3. Segurança e Acesso (Domínio 6 - 10-15%)
"Report on the Fortress Defenses of Domain 6. Explain the concepts of 2FA, Branch Protection Rules, and Repository Visibility in a few sentences. I just need to refresh my memory on these protective barriers."
#### 4. Organismos de Documentação (Domínio 3 - 10-15%)
"Help me distinguish between three key documentation habitats: README, Wikis, and Gists. Give me a one-sentence definition for each in your documentary style so I don't confuse them during the test."
#### 5. Ambientes de Desenvolvimento (Domínio 4 - 10-15%)
"Explain the 'evolutionary difference' between github.dev and GitHub Codespaces. When should an explorer choose one over the other? Keep it brief and focused on the technical distinctions for the GH-900."
#### 6. Anatomia do Repositório (Domínio 2 - 10-15%)
"Expedition Guide, provide a quick habitat analysis of a GitHub Repository. Briefly explain the purpose of its vital organs: README, LICENSE, .gitignore, and CODEOWNERS. Why are these files essential for the survival of a project? Keep the response brief and in English."
#### 7. Monitoramento e Saúde (Domínio 2 - 10-15%)
"Guide, I need a short report on the Observation Tools of Domain 2. Briefly explain Repository Insights and Dependency Insights. How do these metrics help an explorer monitor the health and safety of their habitat? Output in English."
#### 8. Trilhas de Migração e Planejamento (Domínio 5 - 5-10%)
"Expedition Guide, summarize the Migration Planning tools of Domain 5. What is the specific role of Labels, Milestones, and GitHub Projects in organizing a developer's journey? Give me a concise, exam-focused comparison in English."
#### 9. O Oceano Global do Open Source (Domínio 7 - 5-10%)
"Report on the Global Ocean of Domain 7. Briefly define Open Source, GitHub Sponsors, and the Marketplace. How do these elements allow different species of developers to support and evolve the entire ecosystem? English response, please."
#### 10. Estratégias de Evolução Coletiva (Domínio 7 - 5-10%)
"Guide, explain the difference between Forks, Templates, and InnerSource. When should an explorer use a fork instead of a template to start a new lineage of code? Provide a short, clear distinction for the exam in English."
